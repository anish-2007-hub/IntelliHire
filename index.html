Same candidate. Two interviewers. Two completely different verdicts.

IntelliHire reads the job description and a candidate's resume, drafts the questions the role actually needs answered, scores every response against that role's rubric, then reasons across past interviews and hiring policy before it recommends anything — including which of two candidates to hire.

What happens today

Most AI interview tools ask a preset list of questions and transcribe the answers. There's no judgment and no memory of what a rubric, a policy, or an earlier round already established. So two interviewers can sit across the same candidate and walk away with two different verdicts, and neither can point to why.

What IntelliHire does instead

It's one agent, not a form. IntelliHire reasons jointly over the job description, the resume, prior interview notes and hiring policy, generates questions from that context, scores answers against the role's actual rubric, and turns the result into a recommendation with a trail back to the evidence that produced it.

Try it: reason through a real interview file

One requisition, two candidates. Open a case, generate its interview, evaluate the responses, then run cross-source reasoning. Once both candidates have been evaluated, IntelliHire will compare them and recommend which one to hire.
